# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and constructive environment
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive participation include:

- communicating with empathy and respect;
- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- respecting differing viewpoints and levels of experience; and
- prioritizing the health and safety of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or imagery, threats, doxxing, and publishing another
person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review discussions,
community spaces, and public interactions representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly, kept confidential where possible,
and handled in a manner proportionate to the circumstances. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior violates this code.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
